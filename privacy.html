Privacy Policy

Wingflow

Effective May 25, 2026

The short version

Wingflow collects nothing. Zero. It runs entirely on your device and never sends any data anywhere.

No data collected

Wingflow does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-ins, and no user profiles.

No network requests

All fluid dynamics simulations run locally on your device using only on-device compute. The app makes no network requests and has no server backend.

No analytics or tracking

Wingflow contains no analytics SDKs, crash reporters, advertising identifiers, or any third-party tracking of any kind.

No in-app purchases

Wingflow is completely free. There are no in-app purchases, subscriptions, or paywalls.

Children's privacy

Because Wingflow collects no data whatsoever, it is safe for users of all ages.

Changes to this policy

If anything ever changes, an updated policy will be posted here with a new effective date.
